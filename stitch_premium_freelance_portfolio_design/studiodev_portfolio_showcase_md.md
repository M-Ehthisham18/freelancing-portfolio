# StudioDev - Portfolio Design Showcase

This document outlines the visual identity and design structure for **StudioDev**, a premium software consultancy brand. The design follows an "Executive Minimalist" aesthetic, focusing on dark-mode professional interfaces, high-end SaaS styling, and business-centric narratives.

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Language

### Core Logo
The StudioDev brand is anchored by a minimalist, geometric monogram.
![StudioDev Logo]({{DATA:IMAGE:IMAGE_17}})
*A clean, monochrome monogram representing precision and modern development.*

### Design System: Executive Minimalist
- **Color Palette:** Deep navy and charcoal surfaces (`#0b1326`, `#060e20`) with indigo primary accents (`#6366f1`).
- **Typography:** Geist (Sans-serif) for a modern, technical, yet approachable feel.
- **Roundness:** `ROUND_EIGHT` (8px corner radius) for a balanced, professional look.
